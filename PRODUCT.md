## Platform
web
## Product Purpose
Existing bilingual I Ching and Tarot reflection app. Retain current reading mechanisms and local history; redesign presentation and motion.
## Brand Commitments
User-pinned: grey-pink, lace, polka dots, divinity. Seven supplied references establish faded rose, silver paper, floral netting and quiet celestial ornament.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Static frontend; serverless daily and AI reading endpoints. Real AI readings need ANTHROPIC_API_KEY. Local style preview defaults to a separate offline reading adapter that combines preset card/hexagram meanings and labels the source in results, history and exports. It requires no API key and does not analyze the specific question. The original Anthropic serverless endpoint is preserved for the next developer; local AI calls require an explicit ai mode and key. User-confirmed default language follows browser language, with manual switching retained.

User refinement: use lighter warm pink rather than violet-pink, near-white ivory rounded buttons, and remove nonessential small copy.

User-pinned title: always display “Find your answer” in English, regardless of interface language, using elegant high-contrast serif lettering inspired by the supplied Bruney reference.

First-arrival flow: show a separate airy welcome homepage, composed from user-supplied materials, before entering the existing reading experience.

Latest welcome direction: replace all previous collage motifs with the two newly supplied ornaments: dotted filigree upper-left and lower-right, celestial engraving centrally, on grey textured paper. Smaller entry without arrow and smaller visible masthead icons.

Welcome background now uses the user-supplied faded rose paper reference; corner ornaments touch viewport edges and extend under the navigation. Top controls have no borders.
